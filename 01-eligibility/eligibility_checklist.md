# Eligibility Checklist

## Company Requirements

- [ ] UK-registered SME (Companies House registration required)
- [ ] Single applicant only — no collaborative applications
- [ ] Micro, small, or medium enterprise (per UK definition, not EU)
- [ ] Carrying out bulk of project work in the UK
- [ ] Exploiting results from/in the UK
- [ ] Adequate liquidity and financial strength to service the loan
- [ ] Credible repayment strategy

## Project Requirements

- [ ] Late-stage R&D (TRL 6-7 or above)
- [ ] Clear route to commercialisation during or shortly after loan term
- [ ] NOT fundamental research or feasibility studies
- [ ] Economic benefit to the UK demonstrable

## Sector Eligibility

**Eligible sectors (Industrial Strategy):**
- [ ] Advanced Manufacturing
- [ ] Clean Energy
- [ ] Creative Industries
- [ ] Defence
- [ ] Digital and Technologies
- [ ] Life Sciences

**Explicitly excluded:**
- [ ] Financial Services
- [ ] Professional/Business Services

## NOT Eligible

- Pre-revenue businesses with no external funding
- Businesses seeking grant substitution
- Businesses with weak balance sheets or poor credit history
- Businesses that have not exploited previous IUK awards
- Applying for the same project as an existing IUK award

## What the Loan CAN Fund

- Labour costs
- Market testing and real-world validation
- Initial tooling or production scale-up
- Capital usage / equipment
- Up to 20% working capital (customer trials, UX testing, first product runs, pilot sales prep)
- Pre-commercial activities building during R&D phase
- Capital assets including adaptation costs

## What the Loan CANNOT Fund

- General business purposes
- Fundamental research or feasibility studies
- General advertising
- Revenue-generating commercial activities
- Identical projects funded by other loans/grants
- Businesses in exclusion sectors
