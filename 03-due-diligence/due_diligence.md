# Due Diligence — What IUK Checks

## What They Look At

### 1. Company Financials
- Last 2-3 years of filed accounts (Companies House)
- Management accounts (if more recent than filed accounts)
- Cash flow forecasts (12-24 months minimum)
- Balance sheet strength — liquidity ratio ≥1.1x
- Existing debt — type, amount, covenants
- Credit history and scores

### 2. Management Team
- CVs of key directors/senior team
- Track record of delivering R&D or commercial projects
- Any previous IUK funding — and whether it was properly exploited
- Governance structure

### 3. Project Economics
- Total project cost and funding gap
- Why the loan is needed (not just nice-to-have)
- Credible demand — who are the customers?
- Route to market — how do you get to revenue?
- Revenue projections — conservative, evidenced
- Repayment strategy — how will you pay it back?

### 4. Innovation Quality
- TRL level — must be 6-7+
- What is novel/innovative about the technology or product
- Competitive landscape — why can't incumbents do this?
- IP position — patents, trade secrets, know-how
- Risk register — technical risks and mitigation

### 5. Regulatory / Compliance
- Ethical considerations (if health/life sciences)
- Export controls (if defence adjacent)
- Environmental impact

---

## Key Questions to be Ready to Answer

1. **Why a loan and not a grant?** — You should have a commercial rationale (e.g., equity-preserving, matched funding available, debt is appropriate for near-commercial stage)

2. **Why can't you fund this from own resources or commercial debt?** — IUK wants to understand the market failure / innovation gap

3. **What happens if the project is delayed 6 months?** — Stress test your cash flow

4. **Who else is co-investing?** — Co-investment from private sources de-risks the application

5. **What is the exit / repayment path?** — IPO, trade sale, revenue, refinancing — be specific

---

## Documents Typically Required at Full Application Stage

- [ ] Pitch deck / investor presentation
- [ ] Management accounts (last available period)
- [ ] Business plan / commercial strategy
- [ ] Financial model (base + stress case)
- [ ] Director CVs
- [ ] Shareholder register and cap table
- [ ] Existing loan/debt agreements
- [ ] IP agreements (if IP is held outside the company)
- [ ] Board resolution (authorising the application)
- [ ] Audited accounts (2-3 years)

---

## Red Flags That Lead to Rejection

- Pre-revenue with no clear path to sales
- Weak management team with no R&D delivery track record
- Applying for costs already funded by another grant/loan
- Overly optimistic revenue projections without evidence
- Technical risks that are not adequately managed
- Sector is in the exclusion list
- Company not UK-registered or majority UK-operated
- Previous IUK award not properly exploited
