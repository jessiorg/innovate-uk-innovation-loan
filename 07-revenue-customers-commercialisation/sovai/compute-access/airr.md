# Sovereign AI — AI Research Resource (AIRR) Compute Access

> **Note:** The current compute access round is closed. Monitor sovereignai.gov.uk for the next round.

---

## What It Is

Access to the UK's national AI compute infrastructure — including the **Isambard** and **DAWN** supercomputers — to train models and run experiments at scale.

- **Up to 1 million GPU hours per startup**
- Access governed by binding contract requiring no copyright infringement
- Must use data with appropriate permissions or licences

---

## Eligibility

Requires substantive UK-based AI R&D focused on strategic areas:
- Next-generation AI models and training paradigms
- AI for health and life sciences
- Scientific discovery applications
- Dual-use systems with defence/national security relevance

---

## Kanay's Position

Kanay can apply for AIRR compute access as part of the unified application process once the next round opens.

Use case: train spatial intelligence models on UK infrastructure, reducing dependency on hyperscaler GPU clouds.
