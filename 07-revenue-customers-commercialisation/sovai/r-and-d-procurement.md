## Kanay's Fit with Challenge 3

| SovAI asks for | Kanay offers |
|---|---|
| Novel hardware or software supporting compute efficiency | Spatial intelligence layer + GPU orchestration + energy-aware compute scheduling |
| Help deliver expansion of AIRR | Warehouse/containerised compute, deployable at scale |
| Heterogeneous systems development | Own battery + renewable energy + power trading to reduce compute cost |
| Novel AI capabilities | Spatial AI for infrastructure/asset intelligence |

**The energy-trading-as-compute-efficiency argument:**
- Compute cost = GPU cost + power cost
- Standard GPU cluster: power is 60-70% of opex
- Kanay model: own battery + renewable generation + power trading = marginal cost of power approaches zero
- Result: lower total cost per GPU hour than conventional data centres
- This is compute efficiency at the systems level, not just software optimisation

## Kanay's Offering to SovAI

**Title:** Energy-Aware GPU Infrastructure with Spatial Intelligence

**What we would build:**
- Containerised GPU clusters in modular/warehouse configurations
- Battery storage + solar to reduce grid dependency
- AI-powered energy trading to arbitrage power prices (reducing compute opex)
- Spatial intelligence layer for infrastructure monitoring

**What government gets:**
- A novel compute infrastructure model that reduces the cost of GPU deployment
- A supplier that can demonstrate compute efficiency improvements
- IP that government can licence for public sector use

**Market context:**
- H100 GPU hour: ~$3/GPU hour (market benchmark)
- Typical data centre power cost: $50-80/MWh
- Kanay target: near-zero marginal power cost via owned generation + trading
