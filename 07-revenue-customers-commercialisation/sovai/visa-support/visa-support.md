# Sovereign AI — Visa Reimbursement Scheme

## What It Is

Financial support for international AI talent recruitment. Sovereign AI reimburses visa costs for hiring global specialists into UK AI companies.

### Eligible Costs
- Application fees
- Certificates of Sponsorship
- Immigration Health Surcharge
- Immigration Skills Charge
- Priority processing fees

### Eligible Visa Routes
- Skilled Worker
- Global Talent
- Innovator Founder

### Eligibility
Available to:
- Sovereign AI portfolio companies
- Other UK AI firms receiving Sovereign AI support (compute recipients, procurement contractors)

---

## Kanay's Position

Once Kanay has a Sovereign AI procurement contract or compute allocation, Kanay becomes eligible for visa reimbursement. This makes it cost-effective to hire global AI talent — particularly for specialised GPU infrastructure, spatial intelligence, and energy trading roles.
