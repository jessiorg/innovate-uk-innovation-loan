# Sovereign AI — Strategic Assets Programme

## What It Is

Funds **pre-competitive AI data infrastructure** — high-quality AI datasets and autonomous lab infrastructure. The government is investing in foundational data assets that the whole ecosystem can build on.

- Companies can build assets independently
- Companies can collaborate in **consortia with universities and research organisations**
- Companies can leverage external partners

---

## Relevance to Kanay

Kanay's **spatial intelligence layer** — the KCC taxonomy classifying raw market data across energy, metals, agriculture, carbon, freight — is a proprietary dataset infrastructure.

If Sovereign AI's Strategic Assets Programme funds pre-competitive data infrastructure in AI, Kanay's KCC taxonomy could:
- Be submitted as a candidate dataset for public/strategic funding
- Form the basis of a consortium bid with universities (spatial data, geospatial AI)
- Be positioned as a domain-specific commercial data asset that other AI companies need
