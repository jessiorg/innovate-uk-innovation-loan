# SovAI R&D Procurement Scheme

**Batch 2 Deadline: 31 December 2026** (outcome 29 January 2027)
**Up to £100M allocated across the lifetime of the Scheme**
**£250k–£10M per project**

---

## What It Is

Government procures novel AI capabilities as an R&D service under the **Procurement Act 2023 (R&D services exemption, Schedule 2 Para 22)**. This is a **contract**, not a grant. Government is buying R&D.

- Suppliers own all Background IP and Foreground IP created
- Government gets a licence to use outputs for public sector purposes
- No exclusivity requirements
- Prompt payment arrangements
- Upfront payments available for cash-constrained SMEs

---

## The Four Challenges

### Challenge 1 — AI for a More Productive NHS
**Owner:** Department of Health and Social Care (DHSC)
Build systems that unlock NHS productivity at scale. Automating workflows, coordinating care, supporting decision-making. Aligned to NHS 10 Year Health Plan.

### Challenge 2 — Defence Mission Environments
**Owner:** Ministry of Defence (MOD)
Open, secure, modular-by-design AI for platform, field, and dismounted forces. Edge AI deployment. High tolerance for rapid employment of low-TRL technologies.

### Challenge 3 — Compute Efficiency ← KANAY'S PRIMARY BID
**Owner:** Department for Business, Innovation, Science and Trade (BIST) & ARIA Scaling Inference Lab (SIL)
Novel hardware or software supporting compute efficiency and heterogeneous systems development. Help deliver expansion of the AI Research Resource (AIRR).

**Kanay's positioning:** Energy-aware GPU infrastructure. Owned battery + renewable generation + power trading reduces marginal power cost to near zero. This is systems-level compute efficiency, not just software optimisation.

### Challenge 4 — Safe AI Agent Adoption
**Owner:** National Cyber Security Centre (NCSC)
Evidence-based, operational risk management for CISOs. Cyber security + resilience for agentic AI. Solutions on open-source frameworks preferred.

## How to Apply

### Stage 1 — Approved Supplier List (submit now)
- No deadline
- EOI form at sovereignai.gov.uk
- Pass/fail on: UK-registered company, challenge fit, 50% of work in UK
- Aim: outcome within two weeks
- Submit at least two weeks before any competition deadline

### Stage 2 — Full Application (Batch 2: submit by 31 Dec 2026)
- Submit via Flexigrant
- Assessment: 4 criteria, each scored 1-5, must score ≥4 in each:
  1. Technical Approach — credible, innovative, sufficiently specified
  2. Delivery Plan & Team — realistic timeline, capable team, risk management
  3. Impact — meaningful government benefit, commercially viable
  4. Value for Money — costs reasonable, proportionate, justified
- Strategic Fit & Social Value assessed by Investment Committee
- Outcome: one month after deadline

---

## Eligible Costs

- **Directly incurred:** Labour (individual breakdown) · Materials/consumables · Capital equipment · Subcontracting (≤50% of work) · Travel & subsistence
- **Overhead:** Proportionate to project effort. Office, library, admin, finance, HR, computing. Must provide calculation method.
- **Ineligible:** Business-as-usual operating costs · Activity not directly contributing to the project · Retrospective costs

---

## Upfront Payments

Default: payment in arrears against completed milestones.

SMEs that are cashflow-constrained can request **upfront payments** — justified with financials. Scrutinised through financial due diligence.
