# Kanay — Energy, Materials and Intelligence Company

*Data room overview for pitch deck and investor due diligence.*
*September 2026*

## 1. The company in one sentence

**Kanay is an Energy, Materials and Intelligence company.** We source, transform and supply the physical inputs of the new industrial economy — power, fuel, metals, magnets, compute — and we apply proprietary intelligence to make each transformation step capture more value than the one before it.

---

## 2. The investment thesis

For every product Kanay handles, the *generation cost* of the underlying energy is fixed. What changes is the **transformation margin** captured at each step up the value ladder.

| Stage | Revenue per MWh | Multiple over generation |
|---|---|---|
| Generation cost (input) | $50/MWh | 1× |
| Utility residential (incumbent) | $250/MWh | 5× |
| Bulk power wholesale | $80/MWh | 1.6× |
| EV fast charging (250 kW) | $2,000/MWh | 40× |
| EV DC fast charging (50 kW peak) | $10,000/MWh | 200× |
| AI compute (H100) | $3,500/MWh | 70× |
| AI compute (Vera Rubin) | $8,500/MWh | 170× |
| AI-generated video ($0.03/sec) | $108,000/MWh | 2,160× |

Generation stays at $50/MWh. Kanay's edge is the **transformation chain** between generation and end product. This is the same model as integrated oil majors — refine crude into multiple products, capture refining margin on every unit, stay resilient when one product cycle turns.

## 5. Commercial model — Source, Transform, Supply

For every product we handle, three things happen in sequence:

**(a) Source from the producer.** We act as channel partner, offtaker, or take a strategic stake. Sometimes with prepay financing; sometimes with working-capital lines.

**(b) Transform into a higher-margin form.** We toll-convert (4N → 6N aluminium), refine (UCO → HEFA → SAF), install (idle shell → GPU cluster → AI-ready compute), or operate (raw MW → balancing capacity).

**(c) Supply to the end market.** Direct contract sale, market-making on the book, or distribution through our brand.

Kanay captures margin at every transformation step. Kanay makes markets between technology makers, customers, and end consumers — sometimes through strategic partnerships or ownership stakes, sometimes through our own product stack.

**Our job is to unblock the bottlenecks that stop value moving through the chain.**

### Worked example — Elcogen SOFC → AI compute

| Step | Kanay's role |
|---|---|
| Elcogen makes SOFCs (Estonia/UK) | Strategic stake + offtake commitment + prepay finance |
| SOFC plant installed | Kanay owns captive power asset (sunk cost basis) |
| Captive power feeds compute cluster | Kanay owns GPU cluster (H100 / Vera Rubin) |
| Compute sold to AI labs | Direct contract sale, market-making on the book |

Three Kanay revenue lines on the same electrons: (1) margin on Elcogen cells, (2) margin on power transformation (SOFC efficiency premium vs grid), (3) margin on compute sale.

### Worked example — Magnotherm + biomagnets

| Step | Kanay's role |
|---|---|
| Magnotherm makes magnetic cooling (Germany) | Channel partnership + working capital line + strategic stake |
| Biomagnets firm (Manchester) makes magnets | Channel partner: source magnets for Magnotherm systems |
| Cooling systems installed | Kanay: captive use in own DCs / eSAF plants + third-party sales |
| Cooling-as-service | Recurring revenue from data centres, refiners, industrial cooling |

The biomagnets firm gets a guaranteed offtake and access to Magnotherm's technology stack. Magnotherm gets working capital and a sales channel into Kanay's operations. Kanay captures margin on every step.

## 6. Fiscal resilience — the integrated portfolio

A 100 MW portfolio diversified across transformation stages:

| Stage | Allocation |
|---|---|
| Generation (wind/solar, own) | 20 MW |
| EV fast charging | 30 MW |
| AI compute H100 cluster | 30 MW |
| AI compute Vera Rubin cluster | 15 MW |
| Video creation inference | 5 MW |

```
Total capex:         $534M
Annual revenue:      $4.2B
Annual net profit:   $1.8B
Blended payback:     0.3 years
Blended net margin:  43%
```

**Knock-out resilience (loss of any one product):**

| Stage lost | Profit drop | New payback |
|---|---|---|
| Generation | 0.1% | 0.3 yr |
| EV fast charging | 3.5% | 0.3 yr |
| AI compute H100 | 19.7% | 0.4 yr |
| AI compute Vera Rubin | 23.9% | 0.4 yr |
| Video creation | 52.8% | 0.6 yr |

Even losing the highest-margin stage, the portfolio is sub-3-year payback and remains profitable. This is the **integrated oil-major model applied to the transformation ladder** — diversification across products creates fiscal resilience across cycles.

## 7. Why the incumbents can't copy this

Three structural barriers:

1. **Cost-base lock-in.** Established trading houses (Glencore, Trafigura, Vitol) price off replacement cost — what it would cost to find new supply. Kanay prices off marginal cost of running what we've already built. Their balance sheets and counterparty relationships are built around the replacement model.

2. **Capital structure.** Renewable generation + GPU clusters + strategic stakes is a 25-year asset duration. Most trading houses are funded on 12-month working capital cycles. Kanay's capital is sourced for duration.

3. **Intelligence layer.** Kanay's applied AI is proprietary and domain-grounded. The model that classifies feedstock chains, monitors GPU cluster economics, and prices transformation spreads isn't on a Bloomberg terminal — it's Kanay's own.

## 9. Investor bridge (DD vocabulary)

In investor materials, the proprietary commodity trading business is described using standard trading-house vocabulary to allow comparability:

| Public / website label | Investor / DD label |
|---|---|
| Source, Transform and Supply | **Proprietary Commodity Trading** (Geneva / Merc comp path) |
| Energy, Materials and Intelligence company | **Energy and Commodities company** (Vitol / Glencore comp path) |
| Materials & Solutions Provider (to buyers) | **Specialty Supplier** (Materion / Vital Metals comp path) |
| Channel Partner (to suppliers) | **Offtaker** (commodity-market case) |

This dual vocabulary is intentional: investors pattern-match to comps; buyers, suppliers, and partners pattern-match to procurement-grade language. Both are honest descriptions of the same business.
