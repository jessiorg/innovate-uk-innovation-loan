## Key Points for Kanay

### Security (confirmed)
- General corporate debenture: fixed charge over key assets + floating charge over all other assets
- **No personal guarantees** — confirmed in writing
- No security over principal private residences
- IP owned by the borrower (Kanay) — confirmed in T&Cs
- IUK may require priority over assets not specifically scheduled in support of the loan

### Covenants
- Quarterly management information to MSP
- Annual accounts (audited/drafted)
- Key person clauses possible (see below)
- Negative covenants: cash out control, dividends, director loan withdrawals
- Change of control requires prior written consent (not unreasonably withheld)
- Right to require early repayment if IP supported by IUK is lost
- Right to seek early repayment through refinancing if no longer need for public funding
- Right to control unexploited IP in crystallisation event and distribute to UK innovation community

### Key Person Risk
Where the project's viability relies on specific individuals, IUK **may require key person clauses** — departure without prior agreement could constitute an event of default. This is relevant for Kanay: ensure the loan agreement excludes routine personnel changes.

### Monitoring Requirements
- MSP (Monitoring Service Provider) assigned for project duration
- Quarterly meeting with MSP (written report due 14 days before meeting)
- Three assurance areas: technical, financial, project management
- Failure in any area prevents drawdown validation
- Exploitation plan required at start, updated every 6 months

### IP Position
- IPRs developed during the project are **owned by Kanay** (confirmed T&Cs §6)
- IUK has the right to require repayment if IP is lost during the loan term
- IUK has the right to control and distribute unexploited IP in a crystallisation event

### Exploitation Requirement
- Must take reasonable steps to exploit results in UK or EEA for **5 years from project end**
- Exploitation plan updated every 6 months with MSP
- This is standard and consistent with Kanay's commercial plans

### No Finance Document at EOI Stage
The financial spreadsheet template is provided only upon invitation to the full application. At EOI stage, no downloadable template is available — the full IFS application form is completed directly in the portal.

These terms and conditions apply to organisations applying for loan funding from Innovate UK Loans Ltd, a wholly-owned subsidiary of UK Research and Innovation.

In applying, you must:
- Have made your own assessment of the suitability of the loan for your business
- Consent to all searches to determine financial status and creditworthiness
- Understand that key persons, directors, beneficial owners, and related persons may be required to consent to credit reference checks
- Understand that false or misleading information may lead to immediate withdrawal, demand for repayment, and enforcement of security
- Acknowledge that the loan agreement must be executed by an authorised signatory with board resolution evidence
- Acknowledge you incur binding legal obligations and should seek independent legal advice

### 4. Warranties

Key warranties as of loan agreement effective date:
- All necessary resources and expertise to deliver the project
- No contractual restrictions preventing obligations
- No offences under the Bribery Act 2010
- Adequate conflict of interest procedures
- All disclosed financial information is true and accurate to knowledge and belief
- No significant change in financial position since last accounts (unless declared)
- No outstanding state aid recovery order

### 5. Confidentiality

- All borrower information treated as confidential and commercially sensitive under FOI exemptions
- IUK privacy notices apply (UKRI privacy notice, GDPR, Data Protection Act 2018)

### 6. Intellectual Property

- IPRs developed during or as a result of the project are owned by the borrower
- Borrower must have necessary rights to use third-party IPR needed for the project

### 7. Exploitation of Project Results

- Exploitation plan required at project start, updated every 6 months
- For 5 years from project end: reasonable steps to exploit in UK or EEA
- Full cooperation with IUK's impact evaluation process

Compliance required with: Bribery Act 2010, Data Protection Act 2018, Fraud Act 2006, Health and Safety at Work Act 1974, Modern Slavery Act 2015, and Procurement Regulations 2015.
