# IUK Innovation Loans — Competition 2505 Full Overview

## Summary

UK registered SMEs can submit an Expression of Interest for loans (£100,000–£5 million) for close-to-market innovative projects with strong commercial potential.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Competition opens** | Saturday 6 June 2026 |
| **Competition closes** | Friday 9 October 2026 at 11:00am |
| **Project start** | 1 December 2026 |
| **Applications pause** | After 9 Oct 2026 until January 2027 |

---

## Eligibility

**Who can apply:**
- UK-registered micro, small, or medium-sized enterprise (SME)
- Must carry out project work from or in the UK
- Must intend to exploit results from or in the UK
- Must demonstrate ability to cover interest payments and repay on time
- Must show need for public funding

**Who CANNOT apply:**
- Individuals
- Large companies
- Not-for-profits, charities
- Academic institutions
- Businesses subject to State Aid Regulations under Windsor Framework (Northern Ireland — different rules apply; restricted to capital usage and material costs only)

**Single applicants only** — collaboration cannot be funded.

---

## Scope — Six Industrial Strategy Sectors

Projects must focus on at least one:

1. **Advanced Manufacturing** — aerospace, materials, agri-tech, automotive, batteries, space
2. **Clean Energy** — wind, fusion, nuclear, hydrogen, CCUS, heat pumps
3. **Creative Industries** — advertising, film/TV, video games, music, performing arts
4. **Defence** — drones, combat air, directed energy weapons, maritime capabilities
5. **Digital and Technologies** — AI, engineering biology, quantum, semiconductors, cybersecurity
6. **Life Sciences** — pharmaceuticals, MedTech

**NOT eligible:** Financial Services and Professional and Business Services

## Funding Details

| | |
|---|---|
| **Loan amount** | £100,000 – £5,000,000 |
| **Maximum** | Up to 100% of eligible project costs |
| **Project period** | Up to 5 years (includes R&D and pre-commercialisation) |
| **Repayment period** | Up to 5 years |
| **Total loan term** | Must not exceed 7 years |

**Interest rate:**
- During project period: **3.7% per annum** on drawn amounts (payable)
- Deferred during project: **3.7% per annum** on principal (accrues)
- During repayment: **7.4% per annum** on outstanding balance

**New — Pre-commercialisation costs now eligible:**
- Labour
- Market testing
- Real-world validation
- Initial tooling or production scale-up
- Capital usage
- Up to 20% working capital

**Security:** Debenture (no personal guarantees required)

## Application Questions

### Section 1 — Project Details (not scored)
- Project summary (up to 1,000 words)
- Industrial strategy alignment (select one of six sectors)

### Section 2 — Application Questions

| # | Question | Type |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | R&D Stage: Is your project at Experimental Development stage? | Yes/No |
| 2 | Commercial Maturity: Select current stage | Pre-revenue → £5M+ turnover |
| 3 | Cash Position | Cash positive / neutral / negative |
| 4 | Breakeven: When do you expect profits to cover losses? | Free text |
| 5 | Professional Investors: List key investors | Up to 400 words |
| 6 | Funding Strategy | Non-dilutive / external equity / commercial debt |
| 7 | Loan Size | £100K–£500K up to £5M+ |
| 8 | Fundraising: Intended non-grant fundraising plans | Up to 500 words |
| 9 | Previous Innovate UK Support | Yes/No/Don't know |
| 10 | Documents: Pitch deck, management accounts, team structure | PDF, max 20MB, up to 30 pages |
| 11 | Confirmation: Company authorisation approval | Checkbox |

## Assessment Process

- Reviewed by up to three internal assessors
- All scores count toward total decision
- Evaluated on: project proposal quality, financial information, business suitability for a loan

**We will decline applications for businesses that:**
- Cannot afford interest and repayments
- Have not demonstrated inability to obtain finance elsewhere
- Do not align with the six Industrial Strategy sectors

**We cannot fund projects that are:**
- Dependent on export performance conditions
- Dependent on domestic inputs usage conditions
