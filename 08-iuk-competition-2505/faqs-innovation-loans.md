# IUK Innovation Loans — FAQs

> **Parsed from PDF:** 2026-10-03

## Eligibility

**Who is eligible?**
SMEs for late-stage R&D with a clear route to commercial success. Must be able to evidence the business is suitable to take on a loan and there is a need for public sector support.

**Can pre-revenue companies apply?**
Yes. Pre-revenue companies may apply. IUK will consider affordability based on the capital position and forecasts. In some cases, pre-revenue companies meet the liquidity criteria through having raised capital from investors.

**Can I have partners in the application?**
No. This is for single applicants only — not for collaborative R&D.

**Are sales and marketing costs eligible?**
Sales and marketing activities are typically considered commercial activities and not eligible project costs. However, costs that can be demonstrated as "pre-commercial" may be eligible.

## Loan Structure

**How much can I borrow?**
£100,000 to £5 million (or up to 100% of eligible project costs — **updated 2026** from the previous £2m cap).

**How long can I borrow for?**
Maximum 7 years, made up of:
- **Stage 1 (Availability):** Up to 3 years — drawdowns permitted
- **Stage 2 (Extension):** Up to 2 years — no further drawdowns
- **Stage 3 (Repayment):** Up to 5 years

**What is the interest rate?**
7.4% per annum total, split as:
- 3.7% payable during availability and extension periods (on amounts drawn)
- 3.7% deferred to the repayment period
- 7.4% during repayment (on outstanding balance including deferred interest)

**Can the loan be repaid early without penalty?**
Yes — no early repayment fees.

**Is there a minimum revenue requirement?**
No. IUK considers the overall suitability of the business.

## Security

**Will the loan be secured?**
Yes — a mortgage debenture over the assets of the company. However:
- IP and intangible assets do NOT need to be valued
- No personal guarantees from founders, directors, or shareholders
- No security over personal property
- IUK will take a fixed and floating charge over all assets

**If there is an existing senior secured commercial lender:**
IUK will expect to be subordinated via a deed of priority. However, equity holders (including secured convertible loans from investors) are not considered senior secured commercial lenders — IUK would expect priority over such charges.

**What are the covenants?**
- **Liquidity Ratio:** 1.1x throughout the loan (current assets exceed current liabilities by 10%)
- **Debt Service Coverage Ratio (DSCR):** 1.2x throughout the repayment period (EBITDA must be 1.2x of interest + repayments)
- Tested quarterly via management accounts

**What happens if I breach covenants?**
IUK may withhold further drawdowns and, if not rectified, demand repayment and enforce the security. IUK can also convert the loan and accrued interest into shares in certain circumstances.

## Project Requirements

**What can the loan be used for?**
Late-stage R&D (experimental development) projects. Up to 100% of eligible project costs.

**Can I use it for capital equipment?**
Yes — usage of capital equipment in the project is an eligible cost. The amount relating to usage can be drawn down when needed to fund the purchase.

**What if I need subcontractors or overseas purchases?**
There are no specific percentage limits. You must demonstrate why subcontractors and/or overseas costs are necessary, proportionate, and represent value for money.

**Can I have freelancers rather than employees?**
Yes, but they should be shown as subcontractors if not employed via PAYE.

## Financial Requirements

**How much financial information do I need to provide?**
The business financial spreadsheet should cover the entire business, not just the project. Both historic and forecast information are required.

**Will IUK stress-test my financials?**
Yes. IUK will consider scenarios including:
- Delays and reductions in revenue generation
- Reductions in gross margins
- Delays and reductions in capital raising

**Do I need an accountant to submit financials?**
Not a requirement, but recommended. A more complete and thorough application is likely.

**Does balance sheet need to include IP value?**
No — IP and intangible assets do not need to be valued.

**Should I show only project financials or the whole company?**
The entire business, not just the project.

**Can I use the loan to refinance existing debt?**
No — refinancing existing debt is not an eligible cost and would not demonstrate the required value add or additionality of government funding.

## Project Monitoring

**What ongoing requirements are there?**
- Quarterly management accounts
- Annual accounts (within 6 months of year-end)
- A Project Monitoring Officer who tracks project progress
- Milestone-based drawdowns subject to monitoring officer sign-off

**Can I revise the drawdown profile?**
Yes — there is a project change request and drawdown change request process.

---

## Assessment

**How will applications be assessed?**
1. Project quality assessed by technical assessors with relevant expertise
2. Business and financials assessed by the Credit Team of IUK Loans Ltd
3. Final decisions by the Credit Committee

**What is the success rate?**
Approximately 20% — in the extended pilot, around 1,000 applications resulted in ~200 loans.

**Is there a minimum revenue requirement?**
No minimum revenue is required. IUK considers overall suitability.

**Can I appeal if turned down?**
No — the Credit Committee decision is final. If dissatisfied with the process, follow Innovate UK's standard complaints process.

---

## After a Successful Application

**What happens after approval?**
You will receive a link to electronic loan documentation for review and e-signature.

**If I raise investment in the future, what happens to the loan?**
The innovation loan remains in place. Early repayment without penalty is permitted. IUK has not found the loan to be a barrier to raising additional investment.

**What if I can't pay back the loan?**
IUK will work closely with you. In certain circumstances, IUK can convert the loan and accrued interest into shares in your company.

---

## Key Quote — Why Pre-Commercial Costs Are Now Eligible

> *"We expect that 'pre-commercial' activity will occur in the extension period and that 'fully commercial' activity will occur in the repayment period."*

The extension period ends when the first commercial sales from the exploitation of the project results are achieved.
