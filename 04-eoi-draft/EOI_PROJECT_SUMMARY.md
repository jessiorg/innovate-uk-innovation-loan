# EOI Project Summary Template

**Word limit: 1,000 words**

## Section 1 — The Vision (150 words)
*What is the problem you are solving? Who has it? Why does it matter?*

## Section 2 — The Innovation (200 words)
*What is technically or commercially novel? Why can't incumbents do this? What is your TRL level? What does "highly innovative" mean in your context?*

## Section 3 — The Market Opportunity (150 words)
*What is the addressable market? Who are your first customers? How do you reach them? What is your route to market?*

## Section 4 — The Team (150 words)
*Who are the key people? What is their track record in delivering R&D or commercial projects? Why are they the right team for this?*

## Section 5 — The Funding Gap (150 words)
*Why do you need an innovation loan? Why not grants or equity? What is the specific funding gap — what does this loan enable that you cannot do otherwise?*

## Section 6 — Economic Impact (200 words)
*How does this benefit the UK? Jobs? Exports? IP? Supply chain? Which Industrial Strategy sector does it align with?*

## EOI Questions (from the 11-question form)

### Tick-box Questions (8) — strategise your answers:
1. [ ] What is the loan amount you are requesting?
2. [ ] Is the project within an eligible Industrial Strategy sector?
3. [ ] Does the project meet the late-stage R&D definition?
4. [ ] Have you previously received IUK funding?
5. [ ] Do you have co-investment confirmed?
6. [ ] Are you requesting interest deferral?
7. [ ] Do you meet the SME definition?
8. [ ] Is the work not already funded by another grant/loan?

### Written Questions (2):
1. **Fundraising plans** — Describe your broader funding strategy and why the innovation loan is the right instrument at this stage.
2. **Investor stakes** — Detail any existing investor commitments and how the loan fits alongside them.

### Unscored Questions (3):
- Commercial maturity (self-declared)
- Current cash position
- Prior IUK funding history

---

## Tips
- Be concrete, not aspirational. "We have LOIs from 3 customers" beats "we will disrupt the market."
- Front the team — IUK scores management capability heavily.
- The EOI is a gate, not a beauty contest. Eligibility compliance matters as much as innovation quality.
- Avoid over-promising on revenue — conservative, evidenced projections score better.
