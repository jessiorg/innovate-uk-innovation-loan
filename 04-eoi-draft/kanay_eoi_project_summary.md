## Section 1 — The Vision

UK AI companies face a structural problem: compute is expensive, undifferentiated, and hard to optimise. The hyperscalers charge market rates for raw GPU hours. The specialist brokers add margin without adding intelligence. Every AI company — from Series A biotech to government-linked sovereign AI portfolio firms — is buying the same commodity from the same providers.

Kanay solves this. We provide GPU compute infrastructure with an applied intelligence layer on top: domain-specific model overlays, a proprietary taxonomy (KCC) that classifies and structures market data, and sector-specific workflows for energy trading, biotech, and freight. Customers rent GPU hours and get intelligence — making compute sticky rather than a interchangeable commodity purchase.

The first customers are UK AI companies, including those in the Sovereign AI portfolio, who need GPU compute at prices their unit economics can sustain. The second layer is energy trading: battery storage arbitrage and grid services using real-time market data. The third is Applied Intelligence licensing — the taxonomy and overlays that make Kanay's compute differentiated.

---

## Section 2 — The Innovation

The technical innovation is not the GPU cluster itself — it is the energy-aware scheduling system that sits in front of it, and the applied intelligence layer that sits above it.

Energy-aware scheduling matches GPU workloads to the cheapest available power in real time. The UK's grid has extreme price volatility: negative prices occur regularly as wind output peaks; the evening peak gap in England (692 MW on average) creates sustained high-price windows. By routing compute during low-price periods and curtailing during peaks, Kanay materially reduces the cost per GPU-hour — a structural cost advantage that compounds with scale.

The applied intelligence layer is the differentiator that makes compute sticky. The KCC taxonomy classifies companies, commodities, and contracts against GICS, NACE, and HS codes simultaneously, creating a structured data substrate that standard GPU clouds do not have. Sector overlays embed domain expertise — energy trading curves, biotech sequence data, freight lane economics — into the model layer, so customers are not just renting GPUs, they are buying intelligence.

Kanay's TRL is 5-6: the core energy scheduling logic is demonstrated in the existing arbitrage models; the KCC taxonomy is operational in the classification registry; the GPU cluster procurement and customer pipeline are being structured now.

## Section 3 — The Market Opportunity

The global GPU cloud market is projected at $100bn+ by 2027, with UK AI compute demand growing at 40%+ annually as Sovereign AI's £500m fund deploys into portfolio companies. The UK government has explicitly stated its portfolio companies need domestic compute infrastructure. Every AI company in the SovAI portfolio — Basecamp Research, OLIX, CuspAI, Isomorphic Labs — is a potential customer.

The immediate target market is UK AI companies with GPU needs that are too large for consumer GPU rental and too small to build their own infrastructure. This mid-market is underserved: hyperscalers are expensive, and specialist brokers add margin without adding value. Kanay's energy-aware scheduling reduces our cost base, allowing us to compete on price while the applied intelligence layer competes on differentiation.

Route to market: direct sales to known AI companies, introductions through the Sovereign AI Approved Supplier List (being applied for now), and referrals from the UK AI ecosystem. We expect first GPU compute revenue within 90 days of the cluster being operational.

## Section 5 — The Funding Gap

Kanay is applying for a £5m Innovation Loan to build the foundations that make the business fundable by commercial investors. Specifically, the loan funds:

- **First 10 hires** (technical and commercial): GPU engineers, ML specialists, power electronics, and business development
- **Initial GPU cluster** (H100, containerised): the capital equipment that generates first revenue
- **Spatial intelligence layer**: the applied intelligence v1 — KCC taxonomy + sector overlays
- **Energy management system**: real-time grid integration for energy-aware scheduling
- **Working capital** (£1m, 20% of loan): customer trials and first pilots

The loan is the right instrument because: (1) it preserves equity while the business builds revenue traction; (2) it funds capital equipment that benefits from full expensing under the Autumn Statement 2023, improving the post-tax return; (3) a government-backed loan signals credibility to future commercial investors; (4) the quarterly drawdown structure aligns with our deployment milestones, so interest is only paid on amounts drawn.

We have existing equity instruments (SAFE and convertible note) that cover operations to Q1 2027. The Innovation Loan bridges the gap to first GPU customer revenue.

## Section 6 — Economic Impact

Kanay benefits the UK in three ways:

**Jobs:** The loan funds 10 direct hires in the first year, with roles in GPU engineering, power electronics, ML, and commercial functions. These are skilled, well-paid jobs in the UK.

**Exports:** All customer contracts will be GBP-denominated where counterparties are UK AI companies. As we expand into European biotech and US markets, export revenue grows.

**IP:** The KCC taxonomy, energy scheduling algorithms, and applied intelligence overlays are proprietary — built in the UK, exploitable globally.

**Supply chain:** GPU infrastructure requires physical hosting, power connections, and network links — all of which create demand for UK data centre operators, utilities, and network providers.

The project aligns with the **Digital and Technologies** Industrial Strategy sector. It is not dependent on export performance conditions or domestic input requirements.
