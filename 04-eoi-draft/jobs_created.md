# Kanay — Jobs Created by In-House Build

> **Purpose:** Economic impact evidence for IUK Innovation Loan application
> **Context:** IUK assesses economic benefit to the UK. Building in-house — rather than outsourcing to consultants or offshore — maximises direct job creation in the UK. This document lists the roles, skills, and salary bands for roles funded by the IUK loan.
> **Date:** 2026-10-05

---

## The Build-in-House Principle

Kanay builds in-house rather than outsourcing. Every GPU server configured in-house is an engineering job. Every energy scheduling algorithm developed in-house is a data science job. Every customer contract negotiated in-house is a commercial job. This is deliberate — it is both the commercial strategy and the economic impact argument.

Outsourcing would be cheaper and faster. Building in-house is how the IP stays with the company and how the jobs stay in the UK.

## Job Quality

These are not entry-level or low-skill roles. They are:

- **Technical roles requiring STEM degrees** — GPU engineering, power electronics, data science, ML engineering
- **Specialist domain knowledge** — energy trading, grid integration, spatial intelligence
- **Commercial roles requiring industry experience** — enterprise sales to AI companies, government engagement

The salary bands are benchmarked against London market rates for comparable technology companies (Levels.fyi, Glassdoor UK, Hired.com 2025 data). All roles are GBP-denominated and based in the UK.

## Comparison: Build In-House vs Outsource

| | Build In-House | Outsource to Consultant/Offshore |
|---|---|---|
| Cost | Higher upfront payroll | Lower immediate cost |
| IP ownership | Full — Kanay owns everything built | Consultant owns or shares; offshore has no UK IP |
| Jobs created | 17 direct UK jobs | 0–2 UK jobs (project manager only) |
| Ongoing cost | Payroll is ongoing but skills stay with company | Day rate stops; no institutional knowledge retained |
| Tax contribution | Full NI + pension contributions | Consultancy may use contractor structures |
| Strategic control | Full — can pivot, iterate, extend | Dependent on supplier relationship |

The in-house build is more expensive. It is also how the company retains IP, builds institutional knowledge, and creates UK economic impact. IUK's mandate includes economic benefit to the UK — this is the argument for building in-house.
